# OSCP vs CEH: Which Fits Your Path

**Quick answer:**
OSCP, from OffSec, is known for a hands-on practical exam in which you compromise lab machines. CEH, from EC-Council, is known for a broader, knowledge-based curriculum with an exam that has traditionally been multiple choice, plus an optional practical. If you want to prove practical penetration-testing ability, OSCP fits better. If you need broad terminology for a role or employer requirement, CEH may.

**Who this is for:** Students and professionals choosing between two well-known offensive-security certifications.

**What you will learn:**
- How the two differ in style and focus
- Which reader profile fits which one
- Questions to ask before spending money
- What details you must verify on the official pages

## Why this matters

Certifications cost time and money, and hiring expectations vary. Choosing based on your target role and current skill level avoids paying for the wrong one.

## Verify before you decide

Exam format, duration, pricing, prerequisites, renewal requirements and even exam names change. Everything below is a general comparison; **confirm current details on the official pages** for [OffSec OSCP](https://www.offsec.com/certifications/oscp/) and [EC-Council CEH](https://www.eccouncil.org/programs/certified-ethical-hacker-ceh/). This guide deliberately does not quote fees.

## At a glance

| Factor | OSCP | CEH |
|---|---|---|
| Issuer | OffSec | EC-Council |
| Style | Hands-on, practical exam against lab machines, with a written report | Knowledge-based exam; a separate practical option exists |
| Focus | Practical exploitation workflow and methodology | Broad coverage of attack techniques and tools |
| Difficulty perception | Widely seen as demanding and self-directed | Generally seen as more accessible |
| Best signal for | Hands-on penetration testing ability | Awareness of ethical-hacking concepts |
| Requires strong prerequisites | Practical Linux, networking and scripting comfort | Fewer; a networking and security base helps |

Verify any row you rely on, especially exam format and renewal rules.

## When to choose OSCP

- You want a penetration tester or red-team path and can invest long hands-on practice
- You are comfortable troubleshooting under time pressure
- Employers or job postings you target mention it

## When to choose CEH

- A job posting, client or organisation lists CEH specifically
- You are early in your security journey and want a broad framework first
- Your role is more governance, audit or general security than hands-on testing

## Cost, time and career impact

Time and cost differ widely depending on training options and attempts, so check official prices and current offers. Career impact depends on your target roles: look at ten real job postings for the jobs you want and count which certification appears. That evidence beats general opinion.

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | Why it happens | How to avoid it |
|---|---|---|
| Picking by popularity | Social media comparison | Pick by target role and posting evidence |
| Paying before building basics | Impatience | Practise networking, Linux and web fundamentals first |
| Expecting a certificate to guarantee a job | Marketing language | Combine it with labs, write-ups and interviews |
| Practising on systems you do not own | Lack of lab access | Use intentionally vulnerable labs and authorised platforms |

## Recommendation: a decision framework

1. Name your target role: penetration tester, SOC analyst, auditor, other.
2. Check ten postings for that role and note required or preferred certifications.
3. Be honest about your current hands-on level.
4. If you want to be hands-on offensive, build practice first and plan for OSCP; if a specific employer requires CEH, plan for that.
5. For defensive roles such as SOC analyst, consider whether a defensive certification suits your goal better than either of these.

## Hands-on practice

Before paying for either, complete five authorised lab exercises on an intentionally vulnerable platform and write a short report for each. If you enjoy the process, that is a good sign for hands-on paths.

## FAQs

### Is OSCP harder than CEH?
It is generally regarded as more demanding because of its practical format, but "hard" depends on your background. Check the current exam descriptions.

### Can I get a job with only a certification?
A certification can help, but employers usually also look for projects, labs and interview performance.

### Do I need both?
Not necessarily. Choose according to the role you are targeting.

## Key takeaways

- OSCP emphasises proven hands-on skill; CEH emphasises breadth.
- Verify exam format, price and renewal on the official site.
- Let job postings and your target role decide.
